Loan calculator FAQ

How is a monthly loan payment calculated?

The payment is P × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−n), where P is the amount borrowed, r is the monthly interest rate (the annual rate divided by 12) and n is the number of monthly payments. A $10,000 loan at 8% over 5 years has a payment of about $202.76 a month.

How much interest will I pay on a loan?

Total interest is the monthly payment times the number of payments, minus the amount borrowed. For a $10,000 loan at 8% over 5 years that is about $2,165.84. A shorter term or a lower rate reduces the total interest.

Does a longer loan term save money?

A longer term lowers the monthly payment but increases the total interest you pay, because you owe the balance for longer. A shorter term has higher payments and less interest overall.

Can I pay off a loan early?

Often yes. Extra payments go toward the principal, which cuts the interest charged on the remaining balance. Check with your lender for any prepayment fees before paying early.

Is this loan calculator a quote from a lender?

No. It gives an estimate using the standard fixed-rate formula. Your lender's rate, fees and rules decide your actual payment.
