About Sky Spirit

Sky Spirit is a free online lane-dodge arcade game. Swipe or use the arrow keys to switch between three lanes, dodging storm clouds and debris while collecting glowing light orbs. Every 500 meters a boss wave tests your reflexes with a denser pattern of hazards. Plays instantly in your browser on desktop or mobile, no download or install required.
