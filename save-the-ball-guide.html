Save the Ball: How to Play & Tips

A free online physics drawing puzzle. Sketch a shield to protect a falling ball from hazards, manage your ink wisely, and clear all 60 levels across 5 worlds — no download required.

Play Save the Ball Free →

Quick answer: Save the Ball is a free browser puzzle game where you draw a protective shield around a falling ball, then watch physics take over. Every line you draw costs ink (a limited drawing budget), so the challenge is shaping an effective shield with as little ink as possible while meeting the level's objective — surviving, reaching a zone, or protecting more than one ball at once.

What is Save the Ball?

Save the Ball is a free-to-play physics drawing puzzle game. Rather than controlling the ball directly, you draw lines that become solid barriers the instant you press GO — the ball then bounces, rolls, and falls according to real physics against whatever shape you've built. Getting a level right is about designing a shield, not reflexes.

How do you play Save the Ball?

Each level drops you into a drawing phase before the physics starts:

DrawDrag your finger or mouse to sketch a shield
ClearTap ↺ Clear to erase and start your shield over
GoTap ▶ GO to lock your drawing in and start the physics
RetryTap Retry after any attempt to redraw from scratch

What is ink, and how do you manage it?

Ink is your drawing budget for the level, shown as a bar at the top of the screen that drains as you draw. Every level gives you a fixed amount, so a shield that's needlessly long or overly detailed can leave you without enough ink to finish it properly. The skill in Save the Ball isn't drawing fast — it's drawing efficiently, using the shortest shape that will actually do the job.

What are the different level objectives?

Save the Ball mixes up what "winning" means from level to level:

  • Survive: keep the ball alive for a set number of seconds.
  • Reach the safe zone: guide the ball into a marked target area.
  • Stay inside: keep the ball within a bounded area for the full duration — leaving it ends the attempt.
  • Protect both: two balls to keep safe at once, with one shield budget to cover both.

Tips to three-star every level

  • Sketch the shape in your head before you draw. Since drawing costs ink, planning the shield mentally first avoids wasting ink on a false start you'll need to Clear.
  • A shallow curve often beats a deep pocket. Cradling the ball with a gentle curve uses less ink than building walls on every side.
  • Watch the hazard pattern before committing. Hazards often move in a readable rhythm — a shield timed around that rhythm needs far less ink than one built to block everything at once.
  • Stuck? Fail a level a few times. Save the Ball shows a hint automatically after enough failed attempts on the same level, so a few honest tries are worth it before looking elsewhere for help.
