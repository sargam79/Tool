Amount in Words Converter

Type a number or cheque amount and get it written out in words, in US or Indian numbering. It runs in your browser.

How to convert an amount to words

Enter the amount, pick a currency and a numbering system, and the words appear instantly. For a cheque, choose a currency and tick "Add only". Decimals are read as the currency's minor unit, so 1,234.56 in US dollars becomes one thousand two hundred thirty-four dollars and fifty-six cents.

International vs Indian numbering

NumberInternationalIndian
100,000one hundred thousandone lakh
2,500,000two million five hundred thousandtwenty-five lakh
10,000,000ten millionone crore

The Indian system groups digits as 3, then 2, then 2 (for example 12,34,56,789). Switch the numbering system to match the grouping you use. The numeral shown under the words is grouped to match.

Writing amounts on a cheque

  • Write the words starting at the far left of the line so nothing can be added in front.
  • Write the cents or paise as words, or as a fraction such as 56/100, depending on your bank's convention.
  • Add "only" at the end to close the line.
  • If the words and numerals differ, banks usually treat the written words as the amount to pay, so check both.

Style options

Hyphenation (twenty-one) is standard in most style guides. British style adds "and" after hundreds (one hundred and five), while American style usually leaves it out. Both are options here.

Limits

The converter handles whole numbers into the very large values (up to about 10^36), and rounds currency amounts to two decimal places. Without a currency, decimals are read digit by digit, so 3.14 becomes "three point one four".

Frequently asked questions

How do I write an amount in words?

Write the whole-number part in words, add the currency name, then the decimal part as cents or the local minor unit. For example, 150.50 US dollars is one hundred fifty dollars and fifty cents.

What is a lakh and a crore?

A lakh is 100,000 and a crore is 10,000,000 in the Indian numbering system. So 25,00,000 is twenty-five lakh.

Should I use "and" in the words?

British English usually writes one hundred and five, while American English usually writes one hundred five. Choose the style your bank or document expects.

Is this accepted for cheques?

The converter produces the standard wording, but you are responsible for checking it against your bank's rules before writing a cheque.

Is my data stored?

No. The conversion runs entirely in your browser and nothing is sent to a server.
