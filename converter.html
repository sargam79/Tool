Unit Conversion Made Simple

Unit conversion is one of the most common tasks in daily life and professional work. Whether you're cooking (ounces to grams), traveling (miles to kilometers), or doing scientific work (Fahrenheit to Celsius), accurate unit conversion is essential. Doing the sums by hand is slow and easy to get wrong, so a converter that shows its formula helps you check the result.

Why is unit conversion important? Different countries use different measurement systems. The US uses imperial units (miles, pounds, Fahrenheit), most of the world uses metric (kilometers, kilograms, Celsius). Scientists use additional units. Traveling, cooking, shopping, and professional work all require instant accurate conversions.

Common conversion categories:

  • Temperature: Celsius ↔ Fahrenheit ↔ Kelvin
  • Length: Meters, kilometers, feet, miles, inches, centimeters
  • Weight: Kilograms, pounds, grams, ounces, stones, metric tons
  • Volume: Liters, gallons, milliliters, cups, pints, tablespoons, teaspoons
  • Area: Square meters, square feet, acres, hectares, square miles
  • Speed: MPH ↔ KPH, knots, meters/second

How conversion works: Each unit has a defined relationship to other units. For example, 1 kilometer = 0.621371 miles. To convert, you multiply by the conversion factor. This converter handles all the math instantly—just enter your value and select units.

💡 Unit Conversion Tips & Tricks

  • Remember key reference points: 0°C = 32°F, 1km ≈ 0.6 miles, 1 lb ≈ 0.45 kg
  • For rough estimates: Multiply kilometers by 0.6 to get miles, multiply pounds by 0.45 to get kg
  • Temperature needs a formula, not just a multiplier, because the scales start at different points: °F = °C × 9/5 + 32
  • Always double-check decimals in scientific calculations—small errors compound
  • Recipe conversions matter: Baking requires precise measurements, especially with weight
  • Speed conversions: 1 MPH ≈ 1.6 KPH (or divide KPH by 1.6 to get MPH)
  • Volume confusion: US gallon ≠ Imperial gallon (different by ~20%)—always specify

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between Celsius and Fahrenheit? ▼

Both measure temperature on different scales. Water freezes at 0°C or 32°F and boils at 100°C or 212°F at sea level. Celsius is used in most countries, while Fahrenheit is mainly used in the United States. To convert, use °F = °C × 9/5 + 32, or °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9.

How do I convert centimeters to inches? ▼

Divide the centimeters by 2.54, because 1 inch is exactly 2.54 cm. For example, 180 cm ÷ 2.54 = 70.87 inches, which is about 5 ft 10.9 in.

How many kilometers are in a mile? ▼

One mile is exactly 1.609344 kilometers, and one kilometer is about 0.621371 miles. So 5 miles is 8.047 km, and 10 km is 6.214 miles.

How do I convert pounds to kilograms? ▼

Multiply the pounds by 0.45359237, which is the exact value of one pound in kilograms. For a quick estimate, multiply by 0.45. For example, 150 lb × 0.45359237 = 68.04 kg.

What is Kelvin and why do scientists use it? ▼

Kelvin is the absolute temperature scale used in science. 0 K is absolute zero, which is −273.15°C, and the scale has no negative values. A kelvin is the same size as a degree Celsius, so K = °C + 273.15.

Is a US gallon the same as a UK gallon? ▼

No. A US gallon is 3.785 liters and a UK (imperial) gallon is 4.546 liters, about 20% larger. Check which one a recipe, fuel price or car's fuel economy figure uses.

How do I convert recipes between metric and US cups? ▼

One US cup is about 236.6 ml, one tablespoon is 14.79 ml and one teaspoon is 4.93 ml. Cups of dry ingredients vary by what you measure, so for baking it is more accurate to use weight in grams.

How accurate is this converter? ▼

It uses exact or internationally standard conversion factors, such as 1 inch = 2.54 cm and 1 pound = 0.45359237 kg, and shows results to up to 8 significant digits. For work that needs a specific rounding rule, check the digits you need.

Real-World Conversion Examples

Example 1: Travel - Converting Distance

Scenario: Road trip in Canada is 500 miles. How many kilometers?

Conversion: 500 miles × 1.60934 = 804.67 km

Example 2: Cooking - Converting Temperature

Scenario: A recipe says bake at 375°F, but your oven uses Celsius. What temperature should you set?

Conversion: (375 − 32) × 5/9 = 190.6°C (most ovens are set to 190°C)

Example 3: Shopping - Converting Weight

Scenario: Need 2 kg of flour but the recipe says 4.4 lb. Is that right?

Conversion: 2 kg × 2.20462 = 4.41 lb, so 4.4 lb is a close match
