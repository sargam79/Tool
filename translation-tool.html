How to Translate Text Online

Quick answer: choose the language your text is in (or Auto Detect), choose the language you want, paste up to 5,000 characters, and click Translate Now. Copy the result, download it as a text file or play it aloud.

  1. Pick the source language, or leave Auto Detect on.
  2. Pick the target language. Use the swap button to reverse the pair.
  3. Type or paste your text into the left box.
  4. Click Translate Now. The translation appears in the right box.
  5. Use the buttons to copy, download, share or listen.

How to get a more accurate translation

  • Use short, complete sentences. Long sentences with several clauses are more likely to be translated wrongly.
  • Avoid idioms and slang. Say "I am very tired" instead of an expression that only makes sense in one language.
  • Choose the source language yourself for short phrases, names or mixed-language text.
  • Translate back to check. Paste the result in again with the languages reversed. If the meaning changed a lot, rewrite the original more simply.
  • Keep names and numbers outside the sentence where you can, and check them after translation.

When not to rely on machine translation

Do not use a free machine translation as the final version of a contract, legal notice, medical instruction, visa or official application, or anything where a mistake could cost money or harm someone. Use it to understand the text, then ask a qualified human translator for the final version.

Frequently asked questions

Is this translator free?

Yes. There is no signup or payment. The translation service behind it has free daily limits, so heavy use can hit a limit. If that happens, wait and try again later.

How many languages does it support?

20: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified), Arabic, Korean, Hindi, Turkish, Dutch, Polish, Swedish, Thai, Vietnamese and Indonesian, plus Auto Detect for the source language.

How much text can I translate at once?

Up to 5,000 characters. Longer text is split into short pieces and sent one after another.

Is machine translation accurate?

It is usually good enough to understand the meaning of a message, email or web text. It can mistranslate idioms, jokes, names, technical terms and ambiguous words, so have a fluent speaker check anything important.

Is my text private?

Your text is sent to the MyMemory translation service so it can be translated. ToolPond does not store it on its servers. Do not paste passwords, ID numbers or confidential documents.

Can it translate documents?

Not at the moment. Copy and paste the text into the box instead. For a full web page, the web page tab opens it in Google Translate.

Why does auto detect get the language wrong?

Very short text, mixed languages and names can confuse language detection. Choose the source language yourself for short phrases.

Does it save my translations?

Your 20 most recent translations are saved in your own browser. They are not sent anywhere else. Use Clear All to remove them.
