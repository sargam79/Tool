Sky Hopper: How to Play & Tips

A free online jump-and-run platformer. Auto-run forward, time your jumps over gaps and spikes, stomp enemies from above, and collect coins to chase your best distance — no download required.

Play Sky Hopper Free →

Quick answer: Sky Hopper is a free browser platformer where your character runs automatically and the only control is jump. Tap or press Space to jump over gaps, spikes, and enemies; press again in mid-air for a double jump. Landing on an enemy stomps it — touching one from the side ends your run.

What is Sky Hopper?

Sky Hopper is a free-to-play, browser-based platformer in the auto-runner style. Instead of controlling movement directly, your character runs forward on its own, and every obstacle — a gap, a spike, an enemy — is solved with a single input: jump. The level is generated as you play, so no two runs follow exactly the same path, and the pace gradually speeds up the longer you survive.

How do you play Sky Hopper?

Your character auto-runs to the right at a steadily increasing pace. Your job is to jump at the right moment to clear whatever's ahead:

JumpTap the screen, or press Space / ↑ / W
Double jumpPress again while still in the air
Stomp an enemyLand on top of it while falling
Start / RestartTap Play, or press Enter

How does the double jump work in Sky Hopper?

Your first jump gets you over most gaps on its own. If you need more height or distance — a wider gap, or a coin placed higher up — press jump a second time while you're still airborne. The second jump is slightly weaker than the first, which is normal: it's meant as an extra boost, not a full second jump.

How do you deal with enemies in Sky Hopper?

Patrolling enemies can be jumped over like any obstacle, or defeated outright: land on top of one while you're falling to stomp it, which also bounces you straight back into the air, chaining smoothly into your next jump. Touch one from the side instead of landing on it, and your run ends there — so when in doubt, aim for the top.

How is score calculated in Sky Hopper?

Your score is the distance you've traveled, plus a bonus for every coin collected along the way. Coins often appear arcing over gaps, rewarding a clean, well-timed jump with a little extra on top of just clearing the obstacle. Your best distance is saved automatically in your browser, so every run afterward shows you exactly what you're trying to beat.

Tips to beat your best distance

  • Jump a beat early, not late. Because the character keeps accelerating, a jump timed for "right at the edge" gets harder to land correctly the longer a run goes on — get in the habit of jumping slightly before you think you need to.
  • Save the double jump for gaps, not habit. Tapping it reflexively on every jump wastes the extra height you'll actually need for a wide gap later.
  • Stomping is safer than dodging. An enemy you jump over can still be hit if your timing is slightly off; stomping it removes it from the run entirely.
  • Coins above a gap reward commitment. If you see coins arcing over a pit, that's usually a signal the gap is very jumpable — go for it rather than playing it safe.

Can I play Sky Hopper on mobile?

Yes. It's built for both desktop and mobile browsers. On a phone or tablet, tap anywhere on the screen to jump, and tap again in mid-air to double jump.

Does Sky Hopper save my best score?

Yes. Your best distance is saved in your browser automatically, so you'll see your personal best every time you come back to play again on the same device.

What happens when I die in Sky Hopper?

Your run ends and your final distance and coin count are shown, along with your best distance. Tap Play again to start a brand new run immediately.
