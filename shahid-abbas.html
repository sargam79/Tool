Shahid Abbas

Web developer and operator of ToolPond, based in Pakistan.

I am an individual web developer. I build, run and maintain ToolPond, a free website of calculators, converters, tools and games. There is no company behind the site at this time.

What I do

I write the code and the text for ToolPond myself. Tools are built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript and run in your browser. Where a standard formula or published rule exists, I use it and name it on the page, for example the amortization formula for loans, the Mifflin-St Jeor equation for calories, Naegele's rule for pregnancy due dates and the IRS tax brackets for salary estimates.

What I am not

I am not a financial adviser, tax professional, doctor, dietitian or lawyer, and I do not claim to be. Money and health tools on ToolPond are estimates that apply published formulas. They are general information, not professional advice. For decisions about your money or health, please speak to a qualified person.

How I check the tools

  • Each money and health calculator has a "Sources & verification" section that gives the formula, a worked example and links to the official or published sources it relies on, such as the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, the IRS, the CDC and USDA FoodData Central.
  • Before I publish or change a tool, I run it with the worked-example inputs and compare the result with the formula. The page shows the date it was last checked.
  • If a tool does something differently from another well-known calculator, I explain the difference on the page.
  • The general approach is described on the methodology page, and the limits are on the disclaimer page.

Tools I maintain

  • Money: Mortgage, Loan, Auto loan, Payment, Compound interest, Retirement, Salary and take-home pay, Budget planner, Credit card payoff
  • Health and fitness: BMI, Body fat, Calorie, Macros, Nutrition, Water intake, Pregnancy due date, Ovulation

You can find everything else on the about page, the guides page and the site map.

Corrections

If you find a mistake, please write to shahid_abbas_haider@hotmail.com or use the contact page. I check each report and correct the page when it is wrong.
