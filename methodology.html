How We Build and Review ToolPond Tools

Who makes the tools, which formulas they use, and how mistakes are corrected.

ToolPond is created, operated and maintained by Shahid Abbas, an individual web developer based in Pakistan. There is no company behind the site at this time. Every calculator and guide is written by the operator. No medical, financial or other expert reviews the content at present, and the site does not claim otherwise.

How a tool is built

  1. Choose a published method. We use a standard formula or rule where one exists, and name it on the page.
  2. Write it in the browser. Tools are plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Your inputs stay on your device.
  3. Check against known answers. We test with worked examples whose answers can be calculated independently, and with edge cases such as zero, very large or empty inputs.
  4. Show the working. Where it helps, the page displays the formula and the steps, so you can verify a result yourself.
  5. State the limits. Pages explain assumptions and when a result may not apply.

Keeping content up to date

  • Pages show a "last updated" date when their content changes in a meaningful way.
  • Values that change over time, such as rates and tax brackets, are labelled as approximate and should be checked against an official source before you rely on them.
  • When a reader reports an error we check it, fix it, and update the date.
