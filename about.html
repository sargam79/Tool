About ToolPond

Free online calculators, converters, tools and games that run in your browser.

ToolPond is created, operated and maintained by Shahid Abbas, an individual web developer based in Pakistan. There is no company behind the site at this time.

What ToolPond is

ToolPond is a free website of practical online tools: calculators for health, money and everyday maths, converters and generators, browser games, and written guides that explain how the calculations work. It started in 2026.

Who runs it

I am Shahid Abbas, a web developer based in Pakistan. I write the code and the text myself, test the formulas, and maintain the site. I am not a doctor, financial adviser or lawyer, so health and money tools are presented as general estimates and link to the sources they use.

How the tools are made

Tools are written in HTML, CSS and JavaScript and run in your browser. Where a standard formula exists, such as Naegele's rule for pregnancy or Mifflin-St Jeor for calories, I use it and say so on the page. Details are on the methodology page.

Your privacy

Numbers you enter into a tool stay in your browser. The site may show advertising from Google AdSense and uses Google Fonts, so Google may set cookies or collect technical data. The Privacy Policy explains this in full.

Limits

Results are estimates for general information, not professional advice. Please read the Disclaimer and Terms of Use.

Corrections and suggestions

If you find an error or want a tool added, please write to shahid_abbas_haider@hotmail.com or use the contact page. I normally reply within two business days.
