❓ How to Play
🎯 Classic — Slide tiles into the empty slot until the picture is complete. No timer, no pressure.
⏱️ Timed — Same as Classic, but the clock is running. Finish before time runs out for a big score bonus.
🔄 Rotation — Tiles are scrambled AND rotated. Tap a movable tile to rotate it 90°; once it's upright it slides into the gap.
♾️ Endless — Solve one puzzle and the next begins automatically with a new image.
Controls — Tap or drag a tile next to the empty slot to move it. On desktop you can also use arrow keys or WASD. Tap 💡 for a hint, ⏸ to pause.
