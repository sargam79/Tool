Snake Arena: A Free Multiplayer Snake Game Online

Snake Arena is a free multiplayer snake game you can play online, right in your browser. Eat orbs to grow your snake, dodge rival players, and race to become the king of the arena — no download, no signup, and no install required. It's playable on desktop and mobile, and works unblocked wherever a browser tab is allowed, including most school and work networks.

How to Play Snake Arena

On desktop, steer with your mouse or the arrow keys/WASD, and hold click, space, or shift to boost. On mobile, drag anywhere to steer and hold the boost pad to sprint. Every orb you eat adds to your length and score. Reach a score of 120 to become king — you'll move faster and boost cheaper, but every other snake will be gunning for your crown.
