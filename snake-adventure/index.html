Snake Adventure: A Free Online Classic Snake Game

Snake Adventure is a browser-based revival of the classic snake game. Guide your snake around the board to collect apples and coins, growing longer with every bite while avoiding the walls and your own tail. It's completely free to play — no download, signup, or login required — and works on both desktop and mobile.

How to Play

On desktop, steer with the arrow keys or WASD. On mobile, swipe in the direction you want the snake to turn. Eating apples grows your snake and increases your score; coins are tracked as a separate stat. Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty from the start screen to control the snake's speed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Snake Adventure?

A free online take on the classic snake game — collect apples to grow longer while avoiding walls and your own tail.

How do you play Snake Adventure?

Steer with arrow keys/WASD on desktop or swipes on mobile. Eating apples grows your snake and score; hitting a wall or your tail ends the game.

Is Snake Adventure free to play?

Yes — no download, signup, or login required.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes, swipe controls are fully supported on iPhone, Android, and tablets.

Does it save my best score?

Yes, your best score saves locally in your browser automatically.
