HOW TO PLAY

Welcome to the facility. I am NEXUS.

Click a switch to toggle it. Make the CURRENT board match the TARGET pattern shown on the right.

Some switches affect their neighbors — plan ahead. Solve within the target move count for 3 stars.

Normal — toggles only itself
Cross — also toggles up/down/left/right
Square — also toggles all 8 neighbors
Row — also toggles its whole row
Column — also toggles its whole column
Mirror — also toggles the opposite tile
Bomb — toggles itself and all 8 neighbors
Teleport — also toggles its linked partner
Hidden — invisible until nearby switches wake it

Click / Tap a switch to toggle it.
Drag to rotate the camera.
Scroll / Pinch to zoom.
Stuck? Use RESET to start the level over, or SKIP to move on.
